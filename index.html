Hi, I'm Nicole

Your dog's second-favourite person while you're away.

I started Just Like Home because I believe dogs are happiest with familiar routines, real attention and someone who genuinely loves being around them. Whether that's in your home, out on a walk, or staying with me, they get treated like one of my own.

I'm fully insured and vetted, and I'm very comfortable with nervous or reactive dogs. I keep every booking small and personal, up to three dogs at a time for stays, and I send photo updates the whole time so you can actually relax while you're away.
