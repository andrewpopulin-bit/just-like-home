A little about me

Hi, I'm Nicole!

I started Just Like Home in 2023 from my love of dogs and wanting to offer the kind of care I'd want for my own. Since then, I've had the pleasure of caring for lots of gorgeous dogs across Sydney's Eastern Suburbs.

My aim is simple: to make your dog feel safe, happy and completely at home while they're in my care. I keep every booking small and personal, and I'm very comfortable with nervous or reactive dogs. You'll get photo updates the whole time, so you can actually relax while you're away.

Staying a little longer?

Discounted rates may be available for longer stays. Just send me the dates and I'll sort a quote.

Booking deposit

A 25% deposit is required to confirm your booking. Your deposit is fully refundable if you cancel at least 7 days before the start of your stay. The remaining balance is due on arrival.

Nervous or reactive dog?

Every dog is different. I'm happy to discuss your dog's individual needs, routine and triggers before booking. Small stays, solo walks and personalised care mean I can tailor their time with me to help them feel as comfortable as possible. You're very welcome to enquire.
