How to use it

The sheet has three columns. Add a row for each booking or period you are away, and delete the row once it is over.

  • From and To are the dates, for example 3/10/2026
  • Status is either Fully booked, 1 space left or 2 spaces left

That is genuinely it. Anything not listed shows as available.

A couple of tips

  • You do not need to log every walk. Just block out the stretches you genuinely cannot take work, like your Ireland trip.
  • People can still enquire on booked dates, they just see that it is taken. That way you can still pass them on to your friends.
  • Nothing you type in the sheet is visible to customers. They only ever see the colours.
