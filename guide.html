2. Your availability calendar

Your Availability page shows the next three months at a glance, like a hotel. Each date is one of three things:

  • Green, available
  • Amber, limited, one or two spaces left
  • Pink and crossed out, fully booked

It is a guide for visitors, not a booking system. Nobody can book themselves in, they still enquire and you still decide. That is deliberate, it keeps you in control of which dogs you take and who they are staying with.

Keeping it up to date

Message me whenever your availability changes and I will update it, usually the same day. All I need is:

  • The dates, for example "3 to 14 October"
  • Whether you are fully booked or have 1 or 2 spaces left

That is it. Something like "fully booked 3 to 14 Oct, one space left 20 to 22 Oct" is perfect.

Want to update it yourself?

The dates live in one small file with plain English instructions at the top, so it is a ten second change once you have seen it done. If you would like to take that over, say the word on our next call and I will walk you through it. Otherwise just message me, it is genuinely no bother.

3. Google reviews

You have created your profile, which is the hard part. Two things left.

  1. Finish verification. Google will ask to verify you by video call, phone or a postcard in the mail. Until this is done your business will not appear when people search, and reviews will not work.
  2. Check it is a service area business. In your profile settings, your business should list the suburbs you cover rather than a street address. Otherwise Google publishes your home address on Maps.
  3. Done. Your review link is https://g.page/r/Cch0vRdtMHSeEBM/review and the Leave a Google review button is live on your site. Save that link in your phone and send it to clients after a stay, it drops them straight into the review box.

Once it is live, that same link is worth saving on your phone. Send it to happy clients after a stay and it drops them straight into the review box.
