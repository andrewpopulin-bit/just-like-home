1. What you can change yourself

Your site is hand built, which is why it loads fast and looks the way it does. The trade off is that some things are easy for you to change and some need me. Here is the honest split.

You can do these anytime, no help needed

  • Your availability. Add or remove bookings in your Google Calendar and the website updates itself within minutes.
  • Your Google reviews. Once set up, every new review appears automatically.
  • Your Instagram. The site links to your profile, so anything you post shows up when people click through.

These live inside the website code, so they are a quick job for me but not something you would want to be doing yourself on your phone. Just send me a message with what you want changed and I will sort it.

2. Your bookings calendar

This is the good one. You keep using your phone like normal and the website looks after itself.

Setting it up (once)

  1. Open Google Calendar signed in as jlh.petservice@gmail.com. Easiest on a computer for this bit.
  2. On the left, next to Other calendars, click the + and choose Create new calendar. Name it Just Like Home Bookings and click Create.
  3. In the left menu, hover that new calendar, click the three dots, then Settings and sharing.
  4. Scroll to Access permissions for events. Tick Make available to public, and in the dropdown beside it choose See only free/busy (hide details). This is important, it means people can see that a date is taken but never who the booking is for.
  5. Keep scrolling to Integrate calendar and copy the long Calendar ID.
  6. Send me that Calendar ID and I will connect it to the site.

Using it (every day)

  • New booking? Add it to the Just Like Home Bookings calendar. Call the event whatever helps you, like "Rolo boarding", because clients only ever see the word Busy.
  • Blocking time off? Add an all day event across those dates. For your Ireland trip, one event across the whole period does it.
  • Changed your mind? Delete or move the event and the site follows within a few minutes.

The website never stops people enquiring on a busy date, it just shows them it is taken. That way your spillover arrangement with your friends still works.

3. Google reviews

You have created your profile, which is the hard part. Two things left.

  1. Finish verification. Google will ask to verify you by video call, phone or a postcard in the mail. Until this is done your business will not appear when people search, and reviews will not work.
  2. Check it is a service area business. In your profile settings, your business should list the suburbs you cover rather than a street address. Otherwise Google publishes your home address on Maps.
  3. Send me your review link. In the Google Business Profile app, or at business.google.com, look for Ask for reviews or Get more reviews and copy the link. It looks like g.page/r/xxxxxxxx/review. Send me that and I will add the button to your site.

Once it is live, that same link is worth saving on your phone. Send it to happy clients after a stay and it drops them straight into the review box.

4. Your domain name

Right now your site lives at andrewpopulin-bit.github.io/just-like-home, which works fine but is not very you.

  1. Buy your domain on GoDaddy. Something like justlikehomebynicole.com.au. You will need your ABN handy. It is usually around $20 to $30 a year.
  2. Once bought, send me a screenshot of the DNS settings page, or add me to the account for a few minutes.
  3. I will point it at your site. Takes about ten minutes, then your new address loads your website.

Buying it in your own name means you own it outright. Nobody can take it off you and it moves with you wherever the site is hosted.
