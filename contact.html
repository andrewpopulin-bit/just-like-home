Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Most of what you need, before you even message. Can't see your question? Just ask above.

What areas do you cover?

I'm based in Randwick and cover the Eastern Suburbs: Kensington, Clovelly, Coogee, Bondi, Bondi Junction, Waverley, Vaucluse, Paddington, Queens Park, Bronte, Dover Heights and Tamarama. Just outside? Ask anyway, I often cover nearby streets.

How much does it cost?

Sitting at your place is $100/night, sitting at my place is $85/night, day care is $65/day and drop-in visits are $40. Walks are $40 for a solo on a weekday or $50 on a weekend, $75 for a double, and $65 for a wash and walk. Longer stays are discounted, so send me your dates for a quote.

Do you sit at my home or yours?

Either works. I can stay overnight in your home so your dog keeps their own space, or they can board with me for a cosy home away from home. Whatever suits your dog best.

How many dogs do you take at once?

A maximum of three dogs at a time for stays, so everyone gets proper, personal attention and it never feels like a crowded house. Solo stays may also be available depending on dates, just mention it when you enquire.

Are you insured?

Yes. I'm fully insured and vetted, so your dog and your home are in safe, accountable hands.

My dog is nervous or reactive. Can you help?

Every dog is different, and you're very welcome to enquire. I'm happy to discuss your dog's individual needs, routine and triggers before booking. Small stays, solo walks and personalised care mean I can tailor their time with me to help them feel as comfortable as possible.

Do I need a meet & greet first?

Not always. We'll chat about your dog's routine and needs when you enquire, and if it would help, we can arrange a free meet and greet before their stay. It's there if you want it, not a requirement.

Will I get updates while I'm away?

Always. Photos and a quick note so you can see your dog happy and settled. It's the best part of the job.

How do I book, and is there a deposit?

Send an enquiry above or text me on 0452 528 894 with your dog, your dates and what you need, and I'll confirm availability. A 25% deposit is required to confirm your booking. Your deposit is fully refundable if you cancel at least 7 days before the start of your stay. The remaining balance is due on arrival.
