Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Most of what you need, before you even message. Can't see your question? Just ask above.

What areas do you cover?

Across Sydney's Eastern Suburbs — Randwick, Kensington, Clovelly, Coogee, Bondi, Bondi Junction, Waverley, Vaucluse, Paddington, Queens Park, Bronte, Dover Heights and Tamarama. Just outside? Ask anyway — I often cover nearby streets.

How much does it cost?

Sitting at your place is $100/night, sitting at my place is $85/night, day care is $65/day and drop-in visits are $40. Walks: solo $40, double $75, wash & walk $65. Longer stays are discounted — send me your dates for a quote.

Do you sit at my home or yours?

Either! I can stay overnight in your home so your dog keeps their own space, or they can board with me for a cosy home-away-from-home. Whatever suits your dog best.

How many dogs do you take at once?

Up to three dogs at a time for stays, so everyone gets proper, personal attention — never a crowded house.

Are you insured?

Yes — I'm fully insured and vetted, so your dog and your home are in safe, accountable hands.

My dog is reactive or nervous — can you help?

Absolutely. I have experience with reactive dogs and I'm happy to go at their pace. Tell me about them in your enquiry and we'll make a plan that keeps them calm and comfortable.

What happens at the meet & greet?

It's free and no-obligation. We meet your dog, walk through their routine, feeding and any meds, and make sure it's a great fit for everyone before you book.

Will I get updates while I'm away?

Always — photos and a quick note so you can see your dog happy and settled. It's the best part of the job.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry above or text me on 0452 528 894 with your dog, dates and what you need. I'll confirm availability and set up your free meet & greet.
